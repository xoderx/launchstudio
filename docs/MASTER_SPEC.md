# LaunchStudio master specification

LaunchStudio is an AI-powered Business Implementation System. The four core stages are LaunchDone, GrowDone, MonetizeDone and ScaleDone, each with 13 specialists. CoVibeCoder covers 13 planned development positions through one live commander. Q/CUE is the planned coordinator for persisted projects, progress and approvals.

**VERIFIED IMPLEMENTED:** The existing Pickaxe portal presents the Business Launch & Growth Accelerator, Business Navigator, the existing tool directory, the first worked sprint, six templates and a 52-week roadmap. Public checklist state is browser-local.

**APPROVED NOT IMPLEMENTED:** Authenticated project profiles, versioned deliverables and cross-agent context in hosted staging. Follow the release gate in AGENTS.md. After it passes, build Q/CUE as a view and coordinator over persisted records. Pilot Business-in-a-Box, 7-Day Launch Sprint and First 100 Customers before broader coverage.

**PROPOSED:** Results dashboards, operator actions, more academy lessons, connected apps and higher service tiers. Measure time to first deliverable, activation, repeat use, verified action success and cost per customer.

**BLOCKED:** Hosted Supabase verification, migrations and identity bridge. The connector denied access to the designated project.

**NEEDS USER DECISION:** Final prices, service terms, outbound integrations and production rollout. Keep current entitlements and billing.

**SUPERSEDED:** The early 26-tool count was a historical planning snapshot. The later portal export reconciled all 52 core positions. Homepage copy now leads with the accelerator instead of the catalog.
