# Flagship workflow specifications

These workflows are proposed. Pilot the first three after hosted identity and isolation pass. At intake, capture the customer's goal and constraints on the authorized project. At each step, save a draft artifact and its source version. Pause when an input is missing or an integration fails. Resume from the last saved result and reuse the same request ID for retries. Obtain explicit approval before sending, publishing, spending, connecting a third party or changing live data. Keep external actions manual until a verified adapter exists.

| Workflow | Customer and trigger | Inputs and specialist sequence | Artifact and success measure | External action and next workflow |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Business-in-a-Box | Founder needs to choose an idea | Audience, problem, budget → Idea Validator → Market Intelligence → Business model architect | Validation brief, evidence map and business model. One measurable assumption to test. | Research or outreach requires appropriate access. Next: 7-Day Launch Sprint. |
| 7-Day Launch Sprint | Founder has a validated offer and launch date | Approved brief, offer, deadline → Offer Architect → Website & Funnel Generator → Launch Campaign Creator → Launch Playbook | Launch checklist, copy, and a baseline conversion metric. | Approve publication and campaign send. Next: First 100 Customers. |
| First 100 Customers | Early business needs a repeatable channel | Ideal customer, offer, outreach limits → Growth Diagnostic → Lead Intelligence Finder → AI Outreach Campaign Builder → AI Sales & Follow-Up Agent | Channel experiment and approved outreach draft. Measure qualified replies and conversions. | Approve outreach and verify consent. Next: Sales Pipeline Builder. |
| 30-Day Content Engine | Team needs a content cadence | Audience, brand, channels → Social Media Growth Engine → Content Swarm Generator → SEO & AI Discovery Optimizer | Calendar and draft content. Measure qualified engagement. | Approve every publishing batch. Next: First 100 Customers. |
| Revenue Maximizer | Operator wants profitable revenue growth | Sales, prices, costs → Revenue Opportunity Scanner → Profit & Pricing Optimizer → Upsell & Cross-Sell Architect | Prioritized tests and margin baseline. | Approve price or checkout changes separately. Next: Customer Retention Engine. |
| Sales Pipeline Builder | Team needs a consistent follow-up process | Stages, lead sources, consent → Lead Intelligence Finder → AI Sales & Follow-Up Agent → Proposal & Deal Closer | Pipeline definitions, follow-up draft, and deal checklist. Measure stage conversion. | Approve CRM writes and outbound messages. Next: Revenue Maximizer. |
| Business Automation Sprint | Team wants to reduce repeat work | Process map, volume, exceptions → SOP & Playbook Generator → Operations Efficiency Auditor → Automation & Integration Builder | SOP and automation plan. Measure cycle time and errors. | Approve production activation after a dry run. Next: AI Workforce Builder. |
| AI Workforce Builder | Operator needs to assign work to people and agents | Roles, tasks, access constraints → Team & Delegation Architect → AI Workforce Architect → Executive KPI & Performance Dashboard | Responsibility map and quality checks. Measure completion and escalation. | Approve changes to roles, permissions, and active integrations. Next: Business Automation Sprint. |
| Customer Retention Engine | Team sees churn or low repeat purchase | Cohort, churn evidence, renewal terms → Customer Success & Retention Agent → Customer Lifetime Value Optimizer → Revenue Recovery Agent | Retention experiment and a baseline. Measure repeat purchase or churn. | Approve win-back messages or billing actions separately. Next: Revenue Maximizer. |
| Scale to Seven Figures | Established business evaluates capacity | Financial baseline, constraints, market → Revenue Forecast & Profit Dashboard → Market Expansion Planner → AI Scale Commander | Scenario forecast and capacity plan. Measure margin, capacity and risk. | Approve spend and commitments separately. Next: 90-Day Growth Accelerator. |
| 90-Day Growth Accelerator | Operator needs a cross-stage plan | Goal, budget, runway, baseline → AI Growth Commander → AI Revenue Commander → AI Scale Commander | Prioritized 90-day plan with weekly measures. | Approve each external action. Next: choose a targeted sprint from measured results. |
