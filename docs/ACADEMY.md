# Academy

The live portal has a 52-week index tied to the existing specialists, browser-local checklists, and a worked first sprint. Later entries use a shared assignment format. Treat them as a curriculum index.

Initial release scope: orientation and three lessons. Week 1 uses Idea Validator to produce a testable validation brief. Week 2 uses Market Intelligence to produce an evidence map and research plan. Week 3 uses Business model architect to produce a business model and one unit-economics test. Each lesson needs learning objectives, three starter prompts, a tutorial, challenge, measurable deliverable, review checklist, discussion prompt, optional advanced work and next-week preparation.

Onboarding and the worked sample are live. Complete instructional content for weeks 2–3, video, community, and account-synced progress remain pending.
