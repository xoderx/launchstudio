## Cookies

List actual cookies and similar technologies by purpose, provider, duration and whether they are essential. Document applicable choices and how users can change them. Confirm actual browser behavior before publishing a consent statement.

## Acceptable use

Prohibit unauthorized access, abuse, spam, privacy violations, infringement, harmful automation and misuse of third-party services. Confirm reporting, investigation, suspension and appeal procedures with the operator.

## Refunds and cancellation

State the actual refund and cancellation rules for each approved product and billing cadence. Identify the cancellation path, effective date, continued access, request channel and any statutory exceptions. Verify these details against the payment provider.

## Accessibility

Describe the keyboard, readable contrast and responsive layout practices in use. Document verified limitations and the process for reporting and resolving barriers. Avoid claiming formal conformance without an audit.

## Data requests

Identify the approved intake channel for access, correction, deletion and applicable opt-outs. Specify identity verification, handling, response deadlines, escalation and logging. Keep requests private. Do not collect them through an unconfigured public form.
